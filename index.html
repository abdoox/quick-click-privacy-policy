Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game QuickClick (the “App”), published by MobiAlae Apps (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information we do not collect

The App does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or any other personal information. We do not run our own servers and we do not receive or sell your data.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels and stars) and your settings (sound, vibration, language) are saved only on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this data in your personal Google backup, under Google's control.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic or crash data

This information is processed by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Your choices and consent

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): on first launch the App shows a consent form provided by Google so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings → Privacy options.
  • Personalized ads: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads).
  • Other regions: depending on your location (for example California under the CCPA/CPRA), Google may offer additional privacy controls inside the consent form.

6. Permissions

The App uses only the permissions required to load ads: Internet access, network state, and the Advertising ID permission (added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data security and retention

Data exchanged with Google's advertising services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data stored on your device remains there until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
